The official [Internal Polygon/Line manual](https://www.marvelousdesigner.com/en-sg/manuals/articles/47358276422297)
describes starting inside a pattern, adding multiple points, completing an open
line by double-clicking its final point, and closing a polygon at its first point.
It also documents precise length/position controls, mirrored creation and curve
gestures. These remain broader than the initial WeftForge two-point straight
annotation. The page supplies no exact installed-build containment clearance,
ID/ownership, history, file-schema or mesh-separation result.

The official [Cut / Cut & Sew manual](https://support.marvelousdesigner.com/hc/en-us/articles/47358433146393-Cut-Cut-Sew)
describes dividing a pattern along selected internal construction; its combined
operation also creates sewing between the resulting pieces. This supports keeping
annotation authoring and a later explicit topology operation distinct. It does
not establish remapping, new IDs, layout offset, unsupported intersections or
undo/redo semantics for the frozen installed reference.
