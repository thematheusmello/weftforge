`replay_review.py` imports no producer analyzer or solver. Its independent material inverse, analytic membrane gradient, local bending energy differences, seam recurrence, capsule distances, exhaustive static vertex-triangle distances, masses and motion agree with the saved reports. Across 423 states, peak material-axis strain differs from producer values by at most `4.45e-16`; membrane and bending energies differ by at most `2.23e-16 J`; recomputed conservative-force components differ by at most `2.57e-12 N`. Preclosure sewing energy differs by at most `1.07e-11 J` because this reviewer reconstructs the source's repeated substep actuator subtraction while the producer uses initial gap minus elapsed travel.

The control and zero-G branch have exactly equal exported positions, velocities, settings and physical diagnostics at frames 0 through 60. Only measured step durations differ. Reconstructing every nonnegative per-stitch target from the source recurrence gives all zeros by frame 27. At frame 60 the source's maximum remaining target is therefore sufficient to establish all targets zero. Source inspection shows membrane, bend and stitch multipliers are local to each substep and reinitialized there. This establishes the intended exported/reconstructible physical prefix; no private-memory byte serialization was available.

This rejects the simple unequal-side or reversed-correspondence explanation for these original inputs. It does not establish global fit feasibility.

This diagnostic cannot qualify original garment quality. It does not distinguish physical fitting load from finite-solver or contact-algorithm effects, and does not authorize avatar removal as a product fix.

Original peak vertices lie near original capsule surfaces: the one-second peak back-neck triangle is within approximately 45 micrometres of supported capsule offsets; the original three-second front-cuff peak is within approximately 24 micrometres. Conditional neck/cuff uniform tangent extension estimates remain approximately 8.73%/14.04%. These depend on a closed loop enclosing a chosen cylinder section and do not prove a 15% material-axis lower bound. Axis strain also leaves shear unconstrained. The historical e342 mesh has different positions, UV, triangles, piece IDs and masses; same-node historical drift is not valid and historical numerical outcomes remain historical.

Conservative membrane, bending and seam forces are reproducible energy gradients. At original frame 60 the maximum nodal seam gradient is approximately `69.11 N`, even though sampled gaps are submicrometre; final gradients need not equal finite-iteration sequential XPBD impulses. Contact/friction reactions and constraint multipliers were not exported, so total force equilibrium is unobservable. Small interval accelerations or a near-zero internal force resultant cannot fill that gap.

The next justified code question is where the dominant signed material-axis residual lives as contact and sewing couple at the observed neck/cuff regions. A read-only triangle/axis attribution is justified; it must preserve positions, velocities, scalar maxima and these failures. Distinguishing expected fit loading from incomplete coupled constraint solve would subsequently require a prospective residual/impulse accounting experiment, not material tuning or a blind time extension.

Before any new SIM005 executable output was observed, independently calculated original peaks for control frames 0/24/60/180, zero-G frames 150/180 and no-avatar frame 60 were frozen in `expected-peaks-preregistered.json`. The frame-0 tuple is an arithmetic-order-sensitive roundoff residual on mathematically strain-free geometry. Its location alone is not a physical attribution discrepancy.

The receipt is `review-receipt.json`; the standalone checker is `replay_review.py`. Run it on a retained bundle with `python3 replay_review.py BUNDLE`; optional `--source CLEAN_ORIGINAL_CHECKOUT` verifies all source hashes plus its Git state, and optional `--historical HISTORICAL_BUNDLE` verifies topology identity. When replaying a copied source snapshot without its own Git checkout, validate its file hashes separately and omit `--source`. No simulator is invoked. Missing retained binaries and omitted original Git state remain explicitly unverified in the replay receipt.
