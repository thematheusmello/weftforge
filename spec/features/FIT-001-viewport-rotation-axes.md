# Rotate a rest pattern around an axis

Select a visible rest pattern in **3D Garment** or **Scene** and enable
**Rotate pattern**. A gold dial marks the center of the pattern’s authored
2D bounds. Use **Rotation axis** to choose the direction through that pivot.

| Choice | Direction of positive rotation |
| --- | --- |
| View | Around the camera direction at the start of the drag |
| World X, Y, Z | Around the selected positive studio axis, with Y up |
| Local X, Y, Z | Around the pattern’s placed U direction, V direction or their right-handed normal |

Drag around the pivot from the dial rim or from the selected surface.
A counterclockwise sweep of the screen dial applies a positive right-handed
rotation about the chosen axis. The dial stays round even when the axis or
the garment is edge-on. The pivot stays fixed in 3D.

Release to apply one placement edit. Escape cancels the preview. Undo and Redo
restore the previous and new placements. The numeric **Arrangement** fields
show the resulting translation in metres and XYZ rotation in radians.
Right or middle drag orbits the camera. Use **Reset** after simulation to
return to the rest arrangement.
