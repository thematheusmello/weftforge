## Executed edit, history and export

1. Revalidate the running reference process and window. The saved original
   rectangle is selected, simulation is off, Particle Distance is 20 mm, and
   both displayed boundary totals are 1000.0 mm. No avatar or sewing exists.
2. Activate the exact process normally, click the exposed Particle Distance
   stepper, replace its selected text with `10`, and press Return. The property
   becomes 10 mm and the displayed mesh counts change immediately after commit.
   No custom pointer event was needed for this field edit.
3. Execute one `Edit > Undo`: Particle Distance returns to 20 mm and the original
   mesh counts return. Execute one `Edit > Redo`: 10 mm and the refined counts
   return. The complete retained REA record after Redo has the same SHA-256 as
   the first applied 10 mm state. Private summaries distinguish the fresh calls.
4. Use `File > Save As > Project` to save a separate original PD10 file. The
   earlier PD20 native file remains unchanged. This save shows the already
   observed less-than-10-GB warning; Proceed is used only for this disposable
   original fixture.
5. Export through `File > Export > OBJ`, again proceeding through that warning.
   Keep **mm (Default), 100%, Thin, Unweld, Single Object**, all patterns selected,
   no axis inversion, no proximity merge, and no hidden/internal objects. No
   export option is changed. The visible title is Export OBJ; AX says Import
   OBJ. A fresh modal REA selection is rejected, so options are retained in a
   private native screenshot rather than mislabeled as REA modal evidence.
