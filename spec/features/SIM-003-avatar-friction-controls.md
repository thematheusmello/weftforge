# SIM-003 · Avatar and fabric friction controls

**Documentation scope, 22 November 2026.** Selecting the avatar in the 3D view exposes separate static and kinetic friction coefficients in its Surface properties. The manual associates static friction with a stationary garment and kinetic friction with a moving garment. The fabric editor also has a friction coefficient in its physical preset. The fabric manual describes its effect between garments. The manuals give no avatar–fabric combination formula, coefficient units, a slip threshold or numeric values for an identified pair. **Harbor calibration.** Harbor’s six original capsule colliders and its uncalibrated cotton define the WeftForge test geometry and material. The 14-node shoulder reconstruction measures the response to its assigned coefficient of 0.4. To establish a physical value, identify the fabric and avatar surface and independently measure sliding traction divided by normal load across 0.81–2.09 N per contact and 0.19–0.69 m/s. A controlled reference slip comparison also requires an identified original garment/avatar pair, recorded field values and a repeatable initial state. The two documented controls alone leave the fitted coefficient unresolved.
